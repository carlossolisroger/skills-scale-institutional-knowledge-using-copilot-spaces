# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization from security, privacy, and regulatory risk while enabling the team to deliver safely. They review technical decisions, define controls, and ensure that project work aligns with policy and risk requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security and compliance reviews for features, architecture changes, and release plans
- Identify and mitigate security, privacy, and regulatory risks early in delivery
- Coordinate with engineering and project leadership on mitigation steps and incident response
- Ensure controls, approvals, and evidence are in place for sensitive work

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Support secure, trustworthy delivery
- Maintain alignment with internal controls and policy requirements

### Typical Communication
- Design and architecture reviews
- Security risk assessments and mitigation planning
- Escalation updates during incidents or high-risk changes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers on secure implementation and validation
- Partners with Product Managers to assess trade-offs between speed, risk, and compliance needs
- Coordinates with Project Managers to incorporate security milestones and dependencies into schedules

---

## UX Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Researchers uncover customer needs, usability issues, and behavioral patterns so product decisions are grounded in evidence. They help teams design experiences that are intuitive, useful, and aligned with user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Synthesize findings into actionable recommendations for product and design decisions
- Validate assumptions before major feature work or roadmap changes
- Identify adoption barriers, friction points, and key opportunities for improvement

### Goals
- Improve user experience and customer satisfaction
- Reduce costly wrong-direction work
- Provide evidence for product and design decisions

### Typical Communication
- Research plans and findings summaries
- Product and design review feedback
- User insight updates for prioritization discussions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to define insights and validate priorities
- Works with Developers to confirm technical feasibility of proposed user journeys
- Supports Project Managers by clarifying scope and dependency impacts from research findings

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts translate product and operational signals into measurable insight. They help teams understand adoption, performance, and quality so decisions can be based on real evidence rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track product, operational, and business metrics
- Analyze adoption, usage, quality, and performance patterns
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for improvement
- Partner with leaders to evaluate project outcomes and next-step recommendations

### Goals
- Create a data-informed delivery process
- Improve decision quality and accountability
- Support measurable product and operational outcomes

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards and performance reviews
- Reporting in project check-ins and stakeholder updates
- Analysis summaries for prioritization and site reliability discussions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Product Managers with evidence for roadmap decisions and success metrics
- Works with Developers and QA to monitor quality and identify issues affecting user experience
- Helps Project Managers track operational signals, risks, and dependencies that influence delivery plans

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the timing, readiness, and communication of changes being deployed to production. They help create predictable release processes and reduce operational risk during launch.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing, sequencing, and readiness checks across teams
- Maintain deployment plans, rollback plans, and communication steps
- Align release windows with QA, support, engineering, and stakeholder needs
- Ensure production readiness criteria are met before launch

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and avoid disruption
- Improve release predictability and confidence
- Keep stakeholders aligned on launch timing and expectations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Deployment schedules and launch announcements
- Post-release status and incident follow-up communication

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers and QA on deployment readiness and smoke-test validation
- Coordinates with Product Managers on launch timing and stakeholder messaging
- Partners with Project Managers to sequence milestones and operational dependencies

---

## Support / Customer Success Representative

### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success Representatives help the team understand real-world customer impact, field feedback, and support needs after release. They act as a bridge between product delivery and the customer experience.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, issues, and support trends after release
- Escalate critical customer-impacting problems to the appropriate project and operations teams
- Communicate adoption, user pain points, and service issues back to Product and Engineering
- Help prioritize follow-up work and customer-facing mitigation decisions

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Surface product and service issues early
- Support ongoing operational readiness and trust

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and issue escalations
- Support triage updates and post-incident reviews
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and resolution progress

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides Product Managers with market and customer feedback to refine priorities
- Shares operational insights with Developers and QA to improve issue resolution and product quality
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting dependencies, escalation timing, and communication needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams model cross-functional accountability and improve delivery clarity in more realistic project environments.
